H33: measured geometry, plausible metric benefit, unproven causation

The reported leaderboard value 0.2778 is user-supplied, not ORGANIZER-CONFIRMED. There is no authenticated submission-page receipt for it in this repository. Conflicting leader values in the retained brief cannot establish the current leader or predict a new score.

H33's median catalogue distance is 1,964.69m; 5.77% of its dots lie within 300m of a mapped trace. This establishes a sparse halo-sampling geometry and exact subset relationships. It does not establish how much of the reported score comes from geometry, geological information or properties of hidden labels. Calling H33 a newly learned fault detector is unsupported; calling it necessarily geology-free is also stronger than the lineage audit alone proves.

Why the geometry could help

For the shared local DTI implementation, DTI = T / (T + 0.2*(S-M) + 0.8*(N-T)). Here T sums distance-weighted truth coverage, S is emitted mass, M is nearest-truth credit at prediction cells, and N is truth count. The 300m triangular kernel makes concentrated, redundant predictions potentially less efficient than well-spaced coverage. Deleting dots that obtain little hidden-truth credit can reduce false-positive cost. These are plausible explanations for the user-reported changes, not a controlled causal explanation of the organizer score. Sparse spacing alone does not guarantee improvement: dropping a useful dot can reduce recall.

Historical HOLDOUT-DTI, evaluator gems57-pooled-hide-v2, 11,321 withheld catalogue positives: P4 0.07304, 95% CI [0.06674, 0.07899], versus H33 0.00370, CI [0.00304, 0.00450]. This measures recovery of hidden catalogue segments, not the organizer's newly mapped faults. A large win here does not imply a leaderboard win.

The separate historical SGMC PROXY-DTI B′ comparison uses 59,937 proxy-positive pixels at least 3 pixels from the catalogue: P4 0.08116, CI [0.07235, 0.09002], versus H33 0.09377, CI [0.08375, 0.10403]. Paired delta −0.01261, CI [−0.02407, −0.00180]. This proxy is not a catalogue holdout or an organizer score. Nearby SGMC/QFaults traces can be re-digitizations; removing a narrow band does not establish independence.

Can a new method beat the reported 0.2778?

Possibly, but no current result demonstrates it. The deferred supervised residual-ranking lane could contribute new information beyond emission geometry. It needs nested spatial validation, feature-alone canaries, a matched current-best baseline, and the literal uniqueness gates first. The fresh review found that all published TIFFs fail the current prompt's forward-overlap gate; no new TIFF was generated after that stop. We will not prune, rename or jitter H33 to call it unique.
