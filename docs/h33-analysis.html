Mechanism: preserve coverage, remove penalized mass — conditional algebra

Under the requested distance-weighted Tversky definition used by the shared template, let T be kernel-weighted coverage over truth pixels, S emitted prediction mass, M summed nearest-truth kernel credit at prediction pixels, and N truth count. For binary dots:

D = T + alpha*(S - M) + beta*(N - T)
DTI = T / D
alpha = 0.2; beta = 0.8

A new dot changes truth coverage by deltaT and earns nearest-truth credit k. Then:

new DTI > old DTI
iff deltaT > alpha * old_DTI * (deltaT + 1 - k)

Only in the special case deltaT = k does this reduce to k > alpha * old_DTI. In general, one dot can cover multiple truth pixels, so deltaT and k must not be conflated. The scalar nearest-truth credit is not the same thing as marginal coverage.

This explains why sparse placement and deleting redundant/low-credit dots can improve DTI. It does not prove which hidden fault pixels were covered, that all catalogue-near dots are useless, or that catalogue incompleteness has any universal distance law. Hidden organizer labels are unavailable. Reported sequential improvements are consistent with an emission-efficiency explanation, not causal geological proof.

Why H33 leads, and what beating it requires (assessment)

PhD-level summary of the evidence across this repository's audits and three experiments:

  1. Ranking with genuine distal skill. H33-A requires six independent physics (magnetics, gravity, topography, MT/conductivity-basement, radiometrics, LiDAR scarps) to agree on a local strike before a pixel scores; H33-B/C add collinear valley/scarplet chains and H33-D straight conductive boundaries. Orientation consensus suppresses single-physics false positives (lithologic edges, noise, processing stripes), which is why the union still finds structures no single field maps. Our SGMC-proxy measurement confirms above-chance distal recovery.
  2. Metric-efficient emission. Binary dots (no soft-mass tax), ~38k mass, Poisson spacing (no redundant clusters paying FP for the same truth), and the B2 catalogue-distance prune deleting exactly the near-catalogue mass that the live metric penalizes as false positives. The D28 → H27 → H33 ladder (44,090 → 40,199 → 37,654 dots) shows monotone owner-reported gains from pure deletion, and our proxy reproduces the direction with FP mass falling 41,593 → 35,221 at equal coverage.
  3. What beating it requires. Emission discipline is solved and should be copied in form (mass class, spacing, distal prune — not the dots themselves). The only open piece is a ranking with distal signal. Our TMI vertical-gradient ranking falsified on two truth sets; the remaining queue (epicenter-anchored, strain-weighted, gravity-VG) is untested. Any challenger must first beat avoiding-random on the SGMC-proxy instrument (which now exists and is validated) before spending a slot. No current evidence supports a specific higher score, and none is projected here.

Key scientific distinction

Fault traces are the competition prediction target; geothermal vents are not the direct labels. An interpreted discontinuity may matter for fluid flow, but does not establish permeability, temperature, a vent, or a viable geothermal resource. Potential-field contact truncations may be lithologic boundaries or dike endpoints rather than faults. The proposed mechanism is a falsifiable hypothesis, not a remotely verified discovery.
