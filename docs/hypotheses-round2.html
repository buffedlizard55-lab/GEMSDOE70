Fixed experiment-2 specification (frozen before running)

  • Inputs: band 9 (tmi_vg); footprint/catalogue geometry only (no catalogue values enter any ranking transform).
  • Fill NaN by nearest-neighbor from the ORIGINAL finite domain, smooth σ=1, then erode the output support by 12 px AFTER filtering (repairs the boundary-fill limitation noted against experiment 1).
  • Channel A: |dominant Hessian eigenvalue| of VG (both polarities: linear highs and lows), σ ∈ {1.2, 2.5}, pixel-wise max over scales. Synthetic-verified: a 3 px amplitude-5 line scores 1.88 vs 1.02 noise max. (An earlier draft used the least-|.| eigenvalue; synthetics showed it responds ~0 on true lines, so it was replaced BEFORE any run.)
  • Channel B: concave-down-only dominant eigenvalue of |∇VG| (contact edges are gradmag maxima), same scales. Synthetic-verified: 0.24 on a contact step vs 0.01 background.
  • Score: max(rank01(A), rank01(B)) over the valid domain (fixed equal OR).
  • Skeleton: 8-connected non-maximum suppression of score, score > 0.
  • Orientation diagnostic: structure-tensor line direction at the EMITTED DOT locations (full-skeleton rose also reported); STOP as artifact-suspect (negative) if more than 50% of emitted dots falls within ±10° of E-W (GeoDAWN primary flight heading N90°E; Area 1 at 200 m, Area 2 at 400 m spacing; ties N-S at 2000 m — see the mirrored GeoDAWN report). Dots are the submitted artifact, so the gate binds them, not the noisy skeleton.
  • Emission: skeleton ranked by score desc; greedy take with ≥2.0 px spacing from taken dots, distance-to-catalogue > 2 px, on-footprint, off-catalogue; cap 38,000 binary dots at 1.0, zeros elsewhere (finite everywhere; avoids the reported portal range error class).
  • Holdout: shared buffered_component_draw + evaluate_holdout (byte-pinned template, unchanged), seeds 20 (comparability with experiment 1) and 71 (fresh untouched blocks), both fixed here before running.
  • Arms per seed: candidate, ablation-A, ablation-B, ablation-no-prune, experiment-1 candidate recomputed from src/gems70/method.py (same-split scientific best), mass-matched random unconstrained (E1 style), mass-matched random catalogue-avoiding (fair comparator), H33 dots (c55bafc4…, reference only).
  • Canaries: raw tmi_vg, channel A, channel B, combined score — each alone, both seeds; max(AUC, 1−AUC) > 0.90 stops the run as suspected leakage.

Promotion decision rule (all must hold, else negative with no download)

  1. Canaries clean on both seeds.
  2. Candidate beats the recomputed experiment-1 surface on seed 20 (non-overlapping 95% CIs; conservative, stated).
  3. Candidate beats mass-matched catalogue-avoiding random on BOTH seeds (non-overlapping 95% CIs).
  4. E-W artifact rule passes.
  5. Candidate file bytes absent from all 862 indexed registry SHAs.
  6. Spearman < 0.90 vs every checked raster (dense or sparse).
  7. Forward 3 px dot overlap < 0.70 vs every checked SPARSE raster (positive support ≤ 10% of footprint; all verified submissions sit ≤ 3.35%, so any cutoff in (3.35%, 100%) gives identical verdicts — no tuning).
  8. Shared-validator receipt plus single-TIFF ZIP round trip on the exact bytes.
