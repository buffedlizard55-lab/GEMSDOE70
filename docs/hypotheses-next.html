1 / medium Signed gravity–conductivity side order. iso_grav_anom (13), cond_surf (17), depth_to_base_surf (15). Take signed paired profiles normal to a gravity edge; look for persistent opposite-side conductivity and depth contrasts along a connected segment. Covered basin-margin displacement could juxtapose density and fluid-bearing units without a mappable surface scarp. Depositional facies boundary or saline groundwater front without tectonic slip. E1 used along-edge magnetic amplitude termination; E2 used unsigned basement-edge × elevation mean shift; P4 used catalogue-halo geometry; H33 fused mostly unsigned oriented lineaments. This proposal tests signed side ordering and a reversed-profile/null control, not ridge multiplication.
2 / low–medium Repeated cross-profile scarp phase. det_elev (12), det_elev_slope (19), iso_grav_anom_hg (18). Test whether paired positive/negative curvature lobes retain the same side and offset on adjacent cross-strike profiles while an independent gravity gradient persists. A degraded alluvial-fan scarp can be too subtle for mapped Quaternary traces. Paleoshoreline terrace, road embankment, or drainage-bank asymmetry. E2 ranks a one-sided ±2-px step magnitude oriented by RTP; H33-B follows valley chains. Here the statistic is repeated signed lobe phase with a drainage null, not a single-pixel gradient or prior dot field. At 100 m this may be below resolution.
3 / medium–high Magnetic edge-width residual under cover. rtp (2), tmi_hg (3), tmi_vg (9), optionally depth_to_base_surf (15) after semantic checks. Measure edge-normal width at multiple smoothing scales and compare to local source-depth/cover expectation; a displacement-modified magnetic contact may keep a coherent but unexpectedly broad/narrow edge beneath alluvium. Intrusive dike margins, remanent magnetization changes, variable survey flight clearance. E1's magnetic-edge termination follows an edge tangent; E3 uses a ratio of gradient scales and an across-edge depth-index step; this proposal tests a cover-conditioned width residual along connected traces, not Euler clustering, raw ridge strength, or their dots. Related magnetic-worm work in other repos makes uniqueness particularly uncertain.
4 / high Cross-field saddle reconnection. cond_surf (17), depth_to_base_surf (15), iso_grav_anom (13). Detect scale-persistent Hessian saddle pairs whose gradient branches reconnect consistently across physically different layers; a blind basin segmentation fault could redirect both gradients across buried cover. Sedimentary basin saddle, erosional unconformity or interpolation seam. Catalogue-tip graphs and existing scalar-cover/gap heuristics do not test saddle-branch topology extracted exclusively from physical fields. Gravity and basement depth may share inversion priors; do not call them independent without provenance.

Source/availability check. Each ranked proposal uses only bands already present in the locally restored, pinned owner mirror; no new external binary is needed to compute its proposed features. USGS GeoDAWN documents the flight-block geometry, unequal line spacing and variable clearance: acquisition seams are explicit competing explanations, not assumed faults. DOE GDR INGENIOUS provides public attribution-licensed fault, conductance and thermal resources. An optional, not presently executable radiometric validation would require the official USGS GeoDAWN release DOI 10.5066/P93LGLVQ and its calibrated K/Th grids; the listing is reachable, but the sandbox cannot download its non-GitHub binary hosts. Do not label that optional experiment viable until acquisition, licensing and coverage are verified.

Gate before any method run. A candidate must first have an assigned method paragraph and a preregistered comparison to a same-split, visible-only current best (not a frozen full-catalogue TIFF). Withhold whole fault components with a buffer; derive catalogue features exclusively from visible faults; mask visible faults pixel-exactly; canary every raw and derived feature alone (AUC > 0.90 means possible leakage). Then score pooled gems57-pooled-hide-v2 DTI (α=0.2, β=0.8, 300 m triangular kernel), with a paired spatial-block 95% CI and count of withheld positives. Before placing dots, check the surface against the full accessible registry under the user's forward-overlap and rank-correlation rule; repeat on final dots. A duplicate stops the run; no export. Only a holdout improvement and an exact-byte validator PASS can be passed to a separate weekly-slot selector.
