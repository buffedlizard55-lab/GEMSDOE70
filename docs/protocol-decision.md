## Verified obstruction

The inherited shared rule defines dots as **finite raster cells > 0** (`vendor/gems57/uniqueness.py`, `_dots` and `dot_overlap`) and stops if more than 70% of candidate dots are within three pixels of any registry raster's dots. The pinned prior [17GEMSDOE E-proba-multiscale](https://github.com/buffedlizard55-lab/17GEMSDOE/blob/main/docs/downloads/17GEMSDOE_E-proba-multiscale_20260930T044527Z.tif) has SHA-256 `ab0a0a62eecf066a82713b09dd49f0f638a91fa3dd81f54cc34ae89afa3872be`. The local [preflight receipt](../evidence/preflight.json) checks grid/CRS/transform and that it is in the recorded [registry index](../registry/inherited_registry_index.json). Its support includes every pixel of the 5,167,373-pixel footprint. This is a **REGISTRY-MEASUREMENT**, not an ORGANIZER-CONFIRMED score or a complete registry scan.

For any nonempty candidate dot set *C* within the footprint and witness support *W*, every point in *C* belongs to *W* and hence to its three-pixel dilation. Thus `|C ∩ dilation(W,3)| / |C| = 1`, strictly greater than 0.70. This is a mathematical obstruction under the current rule, independent of candidate quality or random seed. Masking mapped faults does not alter it. An empty field has no meaningful novelty or competition value. Changing TIFF names, byte encodings or intensities cannot resolve it. No final-dot audit or competition GeoTIFF can be authorized in this session.
