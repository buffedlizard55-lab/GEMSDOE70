Limitations, irregularities and next-session work

Two shared-tool defects were found and fixed this session

  1. Dot definition. uniqueness._dots counted every finite positive value as a dot, so any nonempty candidate "duplicated" the archived continuous surface E-proba-multiscale (sha256 ab0a0a62…, positive on all 5,167,373 footprint cells). Fixed to binary dots (|value − 1| < 1e-6). Reported in evidence/upstream_fix_report.md.
  2. Forward-only overlap. A duplicate must coincide in both directions. Measuring this session: calib_r13lattice_00904 (206,895 dots), r11_greedy_mp (272,853) and calib_hedgev2_01563_sample (227,507) each have a 3-px halo covering 100 % of the footprint, so forward-only overlap is 1.00 for any nonempty candidate. The gate now requires min(forward, reverse) > 0.70; both directions and Jaccard are always reported, ρ > 0.90 still applies to every raster, and no threshold was changed. Three archived submissions — not one — triggered the old rule, which is why this is a representation defect rather than duplication.

Both fixes are in vendor/gems57/uniqueness.py; registry/template_pin.json records the new hash with all_identical: false. They must be proposed upstream to the shared template.

What is not established

  • No organizer receipt. 0.2778 and 0.44 are user-supplied historical values. No score on this site is ORGANIZER-CONFIRMED. The portal was not reachable from this sandbox.
  • Neither instrument is the hidden truth. Instrument A withholds catalogue components (measures catalogue recovery, biased against the task). Instrument B/B′ uses SGMC faults minus the catalogue (an independent state compilation, but partially a re-drawing of the same traces). They disagree, and the disagreement is reported rather than averaged.
  • The candidate is not promoted. On B′, H33 wins by 13 % with a CI excluding zero. The pre-registered rule therefore returns negative, even though instrument A and unrestricted B favour the candidate.
  • Registry scope. Uniqueness was checked against the 62 single-band 100 m rasters archived in buffedlizard55-lab/60GEMSDOE/registry/rasters, not against every private or uncommitted file. Zero Spearman exceedances (max 0.05) and zero mutual-overlap exceedances were found.
  • Geophysical fields are weak. Feature-alone AUCs on withheld catalogue segments were 0.46–0.57. The four-field gate hurt both instruments and was removed after Experiment 2; this is recorded, not hidden.

Session 3 (2026-10-20, branch arena/a0382202) — irregularities and limits

  1. Offline instruments are not calibrated to the live board (most important). Spearman of reported live score vs B′ = −0.28 and vs A = −0.64 over 18 archived files (evidence/s3/proxy_calibration.json). No offline gate in this repo can currently justify a slot.
  2. Conservative post-hoc veto of S3-E1. E1's frozen rule returned promote; it was overridden to negative after the calibration diagnostic. The veto can only block, never promote, and both the raw card (evidence/s3/run_card_raw.json) and final card are published.
  3. E2 design error, disclosed. The pre-registered "E1 candidate (reference)" arm used the E1 production file, which is in-sample on B′ (0.4614). It is annotated and used in no gate.
  4. H33 domain count. 2,438 of H33's 37,654 dots fall outside the scored valid domain (12-px edge erosion or d < 2.4 px), so H33 is slightly handicapped on B′ against arms that place all dots in-domain. E2 therefore gates on random-within-strata as well.
  5. Name-matched scores. Seven calibration rows take their live score from the user brief by repository file name only; byte identity to the scored upload is not authenticated.
  6. Same-lane siblings. E1 and E2 have mutual 3-px overlap 0.80 with each other; at most one should ever be considered.
  7. Sandbox limits. No DrivenData login and no network beyond GitHub/PyPI: organizer pages, SGMC/USGS originals and the GDR could not be re-fetched; all inputs are SHA-pinned owner mirrors.
