Session 3 (2026-10-20): the supervised route measured to its ceiling

Experiment 5 (H-P5-1, preregistered in docs/hypotheses.md) trained a HistGradientBoosting classifier on the SGMC off-catalogue proxy (leave-one-quadrant-out) over the 19-band stack plus visible-catalogue geometry. Verdict: negative. The model learns the proxy (out-of-fold AUC 0.641) but the emitted dots score B′ 0.0793 [0.0716, 0.0879] versus H33 0.0938 and P4 0.0812 — H33 wins the pre-registered gate (Δ −0.0145, CI [−0.0243, −0.0039]) and the candidate is statistically indistinguishable from the pure-geometry P4 halo (Δ −0.0019, CI [−0.0060, +0.0024]). The ceiling is the label source, not the learner. Two implementation events are disclosed rather than hidden:

  1. Emission amendment (pre-score). The registered stratified emission fell 3,067 dots short of the fixed 37,654 mass on the spikier model surface (NMS collisions). Pass 2 of method_v5.stratified_dots tops up from the best NMS-feasible positive-score cell of empty strata; pass 1 remains byte-identical to Phase-4 (unit-tested). Amendment written into docs/hypotheses.md before any DTI was computed.
  2. Writer-caught defect. The first top-up implementation walked onto zero-score cells and placed dots on the mapped catalogue; the shared writer rejected the artifact (positive prediction mass on the mapped catalogue) before anything was published. Fixed with a regression test (test_stratified_dots_topup_never_uses_zero_score_cells). This is the release contract working as designed.

New measured facts: 31.9 % of instrument-A truth pixels lie within 3 px of proxy pixels (the SGMC re-draw contamination channel, now quantified); 63.7 % of B′ truth lies inside the 32-px halo band (the band caps reachable truth); worst feature AUC vs A-truth 0.565 (no leakage canary); the score surface cleared the 62-raster Spearman pre-check at ρ 0.096 and the final dots at mutual overlap 0.301 / ρ 0.015.

Revised next-session priorities (in order)

  1. Independent truth source. The SGMC proxy is now measured as the bottleneck. Quantify how much of SGMC-only support re-draws QFaults (buffer tests 1–6 px), then obtain a genuinely independent additional-fault set (Nevada Bureau of Mines county maps, published INGENIOUS trace exports) before any further supervised attempt.
  2. Band widening. 36.3 % of B′ truth lies beyond 32 px from the catalogue. A registered experiment widening D_MAX (48/64 px) at fixed mass with the same stratified emission could recover out-of-band truth; uniqueness must be re-checked (a wider domain raises exposure to the dense lattice rasters).
  3. Mass calibration. Every fixed-mass family member clusters at B′ 0.08–0.09. A registered dot-count sweep (e.g. 25k / 37k / 50k) on the existing surface, scored only on B′ LOQO, would measure the mass response without new modelling.
  4. Upstream the two uniqueness fixes to the shared template and run its suite (still pending from session 2).
  5. Selector step unchanged. No weekly slot has been used by any session of this repository.

Repository-contract change this session (not a template change)

src/gems70/release.py now reads the canonical evidence/run_card.json and resolves its phase receipts through the card's phase_evidence mapping (dot count from the card instead of a hard-coded 37,654). Fail-closed semantics are unchanged and covered by the same tests.

Two shared-tool defects were found and fixed (session 2)

  1. Dot definition. uniqueness._dots counted every finite positive value as a dot, so any nonempty candidate "duplicated" the archived continuous surface E-proba-multiscale (sha256 ab0a0a62…, positive on all 5,167,373 footprint cells). Fixed to binary dots (|value − 1| < 1e-6). Reported in evidence/upstream_fix_report.md.
  2. Forward-only overlap. A duplicate must coincide in both directions. Measuring this session: calib_r13lattice_00904 (206,895 dots), r11_greedy_mp (272,853) and calib_hedgev2_01563_sample (227,507) each have a 3-px halo covering 100 % of the footprint, so forward-only overlap is 1.00 for any nonempty candidate. The gate now requires min(forward, reverse) > 0.70; both directions and Jaccard are always reported, ρ > 0.90 still applies to every raster, and no threshold was changed. Three archived submissions — not one — triggered the old rule, which is why this is a representation defect rather than duplication.

Both fixes are in vendor/gems57/uniqueness.py; registry/template_pin.json records the new hash with all_identical: false. They must be proposed upstream to the shared template.

What is not established

  • No organizer receipt. 0.2778 and 0.44 are user-supplied historical values. No score on this site is ORGANIZER-CONFIRMED. The portal was not reachable from this sandbox.
  • Neither instrument is the hidden truth. Instrument A withholds catalogue components (measures catalogue recovery, biased against the task). Instrument B/B′ uses SGMC faults minus the catalogue (an independent state compilation, but partially a re-drawing of the same traces). They disagree, and the disagreement is reported rather than averaged.
  • The candidate is not promoted. On B′, H33 wins by 13 % with a CI excluding zero. The pre-registered rule therefore returns negative, even though instrument A and unrestricted B favour the candidate.
  • Registry scope. Uniqueness was checked against the 62 single-band 100 m rasters archived in buffedlizard55-lab/60GEMSDOE/registry/rasters, not against every private or uncommitted file. Zero Spearman exceedances (max 0.05) and zero mutual-overlap exceedances were found.
  • Geophysical fields are weak. Feature-alone AUCs on withheld catalogue segments were 0.46–0.57. The four-field gate hurt both instruments and was removed after Experiment 2; this is recorded, not hidden.
