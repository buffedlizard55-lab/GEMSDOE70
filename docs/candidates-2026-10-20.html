Validated in this session: C1 (pre-registered parameters)

Structure tensor of visible traces: T = gaussian(visible, sigma=3 px); gradient (gy, gx); J = gaussian([gx², gy², gx·gy], sigma=10 px); trace-normal direction theta_n = 0.5·atan2(2·Jxy, Jxx−Jyy); coherence coh = sqrt((Jxx−Jyy)²+4·Jxy²)/(Jxx+Jyy+eps).

Magnetic evidence: M = |∇ gaussian(rtp, 1.5 px)|; magnetic gradient direction phi_m.

Alignment A = |cos(phi_m − theta_n)| (magnetic gradient parallel to trace normal ⇒ edge parallel to strike).

Catalogue proximity W = exp(−d_visible / 20 px) (2 km decay).

Candidate field (before output transform) F = rank(W)·A·rank(M)·coh, then surface = upper_surface(rank(F), valid, cutoff=0.98) (same output transform as the stopped run, so results are comparable).

Arms: candidate (C1); ablation_no_catalogue (A·rank(M)·coh, no visible catalogue at all); ablation_no_alignment (rank(W)·rank(M)·coh); random_control (same positive count, seed fixed).

Holdout blocks: buffered_component_draw(seed=71, buffer_px=3, boundary_px=12) — a fresh seed, not the seed-20 blocks already reported in evidence/holdout.json.
