What the new model tested — and why we stopped.

The pre-registered provisional lane is cover-conditioned magnetic analytic-signal width within the visible-fault halo. The 19-band owner-mirrored stack supplied horizontal and vertical magnetic gradients, gravity anomaly and basement depth. The SGMC fault raster was used only as proxy training labels, never as a final predictor or export mask. Four spatial quadrants were held out in turn. For catalogue recovery, whole connected components were hidden with a 3-pixel feature buffer and visible faults were masked pixel-exactly by the shared evaluator.

Physical hypothesis: a covered fault can juxtapose magnetized lithologies under basin fill; magnetic edge width might preferentially rank blind structures. Counterexample: a buried volcanic dike or remanence boundary, or airborne survey leveling, can generate a similar magnetic signature without fault displacement. None of these pixels proves a geothermal vent.
