## 1. Why Phase-4 failed Instrument B′ (measured diagnosis before Phase-5)

Byte-level decomposition of Phase-4 (`37,654` dots) against H33 (`37,654` dots) across Euclidean distance shells $d_{\text{cat}}$ from `existing_faults.tif` reveals two structural defects in Phase-4:

1. **Inner-shell crowding ($2.4 \le d_{\text{cat}} < 3.0$ px) and outer-domain truncation ($d_{\text{cat}} > 32$ px):**
   Phase-4 hard-coded `D_MIN = 2.4, D_MAX = 32.0` with a monotonically decreasing distance weight $1 + 0.8\exp(-(d-2.4)/6)$. Consequently, every `7 × 7` block intersecting the inner boundary placed its `argmax` dot in $2.4 \le d_{\text{cat}} < 3.0$ px (**7,195 dots**, 19.1% of the budget), while placing only **16 dots** at $d_{\text{cat}} \ge 32$ px where **22,543 pixels (36.0%)** of the Instrument B′ truth (`sgmc & ~cat & (d_cat >= 3)`) reside.
2. **Zero physical features inside `7 × 7` blocks:**
   Within every distance shell $[3, 6)$, $[6, 15)$, $[15, 32)$, Phase-4 ignored all geophysical, topographic, LiDAR scarp, and radiometric layers, earning only `0.044–0.067` credit/dot versus H33's `0.063–0.089` credit/dot.

## 3. Pre-Registered Phase-5 Protocol & Gates

1. **Leakage Canary (Protocol Rule 4):**
   Every raw input band and derived catalogue/hydrothermal feature (49 features total) is tested individually on the held-out catalogue positives (`11,321` withheld pixels vs `100,000` random negatives) AND on the SGMC off-catalogue proxy (`59,937` positives vs `100,000` negatives). Any feature with $\max(\text{AUC}, 1-\text{AUC}) > 0.90$ triggers an immediate leakage stop.
2. **Spatial Separation in Leave-One-Quadrant-Out (LOQO) Training:**
   - The footprint is partitioned into the 4 standard label-blind spatial quadrants (`NW, NE, SW, SE`) via `holdout.quadrant_ids(fp)`, with each quadrant eroded by `12 px` (`1.2 km`) via `ndi.binary_erosion(quad == k, iterations=12)`.
   - For each fold $k \in \{0, 1, 2, 3\}$, the classifier `HistGradientBoostingClassifier(max_iter=80, max_leaf_nodes=31, min_samples_leaf=100, l2_regularization=10.0, random_state=70500+k)` is trained **strictly** on pixels in the other three eroded quadrants $\bigcup_{j \ne k} \text{erode}(\text{quad} == j, 12)$ at $d_{\text{cat}} \ge 3.0$ px (`3:1` negative-to-positive subsampling).
   - Minimum Euclidean distance between any training pixel and any scored test pixel in `erode(quad == k, 12)` is $\ge 24\text{ px} = 2.4\text{ km}$ ($8\times$ the 300 m kernel radius).
   - On Instrument A (`buffered_component_draw`, seed=20, buffer=3 px, boundary=12 px), all catalogue-geometry features are computed strictly from `fold['visible']`, and the 3-px collar around `fold['hidden_all']` is also excluded from training in all quadrants.
3. **Pre-Registered Arms & Ablations (all emitting exactly `37,654` binary dots off-catalogue):**
   - `candidate` (`H-P5-1` + `H-P5-2` hydrothermal feature): $(0.35 + 0.65 R_{\text{LOQO}})(1 + 0.5 e^{-(d-3)/10})(0.75 + 0.25 K_{\text{tip}})$ on $d \ge 3.0$ px, `7 × 7` block argmax, `5.0 px` NMS.
   - `ablation_no_hydro`: Same LOQO model trained without the 2 hydrothermal conduit features (`d_hydro`, `K_hydro`), testing the marginal contribution of `H-P5-2`.
   - `ablation_loqo_only`: Pure LOQO rank $R_{\text{LOQO}}$ on $d \ge 3.0$ px without the multiplicative halo/tip factor, `7 × 7` block argmax, `5.0 px` NMS.
   - `ablation_halo_only`: Pure unsupervised halo/tip factor $(1 + 0.5 e^{-(d-3)/10})(0.75 + 0.25 K_{\text{tip}})$ on $d \ge 3.0$ px without the LOQO model, `7 × 7` block argmax, `5.0 px` NMS.
   - `p4_reference`: Frozen Phase-4 artifact (`gems70-p4-stratified-halo-37654-20261020.tif`).
   - `h33_reference`: Archived H33 artifact (`gemsdoe32-h33-h33-2-b2-20261004T220000Z-e5eb6e7e-zeros.tif`).
   - `random_control`: Mass-matched 37,654 binary random off-catalogue dots.
4. **Evaluation Instruments & Promotion Gates:**
   - **Instrument A:** Shared hide-and-recover catalogue holdout (`11,321` withheld positive pixels, 1,000-draw 20 km paired spatial-cluster bootstrap). Gate A: `candidate DTI >= h33_reference DTI` AND paired $\Delta(\text{candidate} - \text{random\_control})$ 95% CI lower bound $> 0$.
   - **Instrument B′:** SGMC off-catalogue proxy at $d_{\text{cat}} \ge 3.0$ px (`59,937` positive pixels, strictly out-of-fold LOQO). Gate B′: paired $\Delta(\text{candidate} - \text{h33\_reference})$ 95% CI lower bound $> 0$ AND paired $\Delta(\text{candidate} - \text{p4\_reference})$ 95% CI lower bound $> 0$.
   - **Instrument B″:** SGMC off-catalogue proxy outside the 600 m cartographic duplication band at $d_{\text{cat}} \ge 6.0$ px (`51,636` positive pixels, strictly out-of-fold LOQO). Gate B″: paired $\Delta(\text{candidate} - \text{h33\_reference})$ 95% CI lower bound $> 0$.
   - **Uniqueness Gate:** Checked on BOTH the continuous score surface before placement AND the final 37,654 binary dots against all 62 archived registry rasters (plus all 4 prior `GEMSDOE70` rasters): Spearman $\rho \le 0.90$, mutual 3-px dot overlap $\le 0.70$, Jaccard $\le 0.50$.
   - **Selector Rule (Protocol Rule 6 & AGENTS.md Rule 6):** Clearing all gates promotes the Phase-5 candidate to `selector_ready = true` (`verdict = "promote"` in `evidence/phase5/run_card.json`), while `weekly_slots_used = 0` and the frozen Phase-4 release contract (`evidence/run_card.json`) remains untouched so all regression gates pass.
