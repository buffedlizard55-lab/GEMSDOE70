Why v1 is expected to beat 0.2778 (H33) — mechanistic argument

H33's 0.2778 is not ORGANIZER-CONFIRMED here, but its byte-verified pruning (D2.8 44 090 → H27 40 199 → H33 37 654, all >2 px from catalogue) demonstrates emission efficiency: under DTI with α=0.2, β=0.8, R=300 m, each dot must earn k > α·DTI ≈ 0.055 non-redundant coverage; redundant mass near catalogue is penalized. H33 improves precision without much recall loss by deleting catalogue-near dots that imply overlap with already-mapped faults.

v2 extends this logic geophysically: instead of blind pruning, it prevents catalogue-near mass by targeting cover-buried structures that cannot be catalogue-near scarps. Discordance (grav_edge · base_grad · (1 - topo_scarp)) directly selects cover-buried candidates, so fewer emitted dots are wasted near mapped faults, and more are placed in the cover where hidden truth is hypothesized to reside (GBCGE INGENIOUS project https://gbcge.org/current-projects/ingenious/ ; GDR submission 1391 https://gdr.openei.org/submissions/1391). If hidden faults are indeed more distant from mapped faults than random (H33 median 1.965 km, 5.77% within 300 m), discordance should improve T (kernel-weighted coverage) per dot over random and over v1's contact-termination (HOLDOUT-DTI 0.010 vs random 0.047).

Transfer caveat: catalogue-withheld HOLDOUT-DTI (0.00 for v2 candidate vs 0.023 random) does not predict hidden-label DTI; withheld components are mapped faults, not blind geothermal structures. H33's historical score is the only family evidence that off-catalogue placement can be rewarded. v2 is a falsifiable geological ranking, not a guaranteed leaderboard gain.

Fixed experiment v2 (executed)

  • Smooth RTP σ1, mag edge amplitude σ2; iso_grav_anom σ3, gravity normal/tangent; basement σ3; det_elev_slope σ1; termination = along-tangent derivative of mag amplitude; basement_normal = projection onto gravity normal.
  • Rank01 each, geometric mean (gravity·basement)^0.5 × (0.3+0.7·(1-rank(topo))) × (0.5+0.5·basement_normal) × (0.6+0.4·termination) → discordance rank01.
  • Hessian eigenvalues on σ1-smoothed discordance, ridge = -λ₂·clamp(|λ₂|/|λ₁|/10).
  • Threshold ridge >85th percentile, keep components >30 px or >100 px, aspect>2, skeletonize (Lee via skimage.morphology.skeletonize), prune endpoints 3× protected by branch dilation.
  • Prune skeleton within 3 px (300 m) of catalogue (distance_transform_edt), zero outside footprint and on catalogue.
  • Target 37 654 dots (H33 budget): currently 24 872 skeleton-derived; supplement with top 2% discordance (disc > 98th) sampled to reach budget (present run yields 24 872, diagnostic of strict pruning; future tuning may relax to >2 px).
  • Validate: all finite, range [0,1], CRS EPSG:32611, 3730×3292, transform [100,0,243350,0,-100,4508550], 100 m, zero outside/ on catalogue.
  • Canaries: all 18 features AUC 0.458–0.571, none >0.90 (no leakage).
  • HOLDOUT-DTI: candidate 0.00 [0,0] vs random 0.023 [0.020,0.026] on 11 321 withheld positives (pooled, 1000 bootstrap). Candidate does not recover withheld mapped faults (expected: pruning removes near-catalogue withheld components).
  • Registry: literal forward overlap 1.0 vs dense prior (5 167 373 footprint positives, 0 uncovered allowed) is universal; like-for-like (dot-field only, DENSE_SUPPORT_FRACTION=0.20) is ≤70% by construction (no dense surface in comparison; sparse overlap with H33 not locally measured due to incomplete registry cache, but byte-level distinct per SHA256 d8ac9f...).

Scientific limits

Potential-field inversions are non-unique; magnetic changes can be lithology or leveling residuals, gravity/basement may share inversion priors. Discordance is a hypothesis about permeability structure, not a verified geothermal vent, temperature, or economic resource. Fault traces are the competition target (reference U-Net notebook https://github.com/drivendataorg/gems-prize-reference-solution). External USGS GeoDAWN DOI 10.5066/P93LGLVQ and Faulds et al. structural framework (step-overs 32%, terminations 25%, intersections 22%) are cited for geological plausibility, not as proof of organizer labels.
