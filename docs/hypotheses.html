Fixed first experiment

No external-data-dependent idea is asserted viable. All named layers are already obtainable in the restored, SHA-pinned owner mirror. Their descriptions are metadata, not independent proof of the precise inversion or units.

  • Smooth RTP with Gaussian sigma one pixel; compute magnetic gradient magnitude, then smooth its amplitude with sigma two pixels.
  • Smooth gravity anomaly with sigma three pixels; compute its unit normal and tangent.
  • Feature termination: absolute directional derivative of magnetic edge amplitude along that tangent.
  • Feature gravity_edge: magnitude of the smoothed gravity gradient.
  • Feature basement_normal: absolute projection of the sigma-three basement-depth gradient onto the gravity normal.
  • Rank each feature with average ties over the finite feature domain (no catalogue input). Candidate field is the geometric mean of these three ranks. Ablation omits termination.
  • Continuous evaluation field retains the upper two percent of ranks, linearly rescaled to [0,1]. This is a fixed surface transform, not final dot placement, not a calibrated probability.
  • Test every raw input and derived feature alone with holdout AUC and HOLDOUT-DTI before interpreting the joint field. AUC > 0.90 stops the run as suspected leakage.
  • Shared buffered_component_draw: seed twenty, whole connected catalogue components withheld in four spatial quadrants, three-pixel catalogue feature collar and twelve-pixel boundary erosion. No catalogue-derived predictor is used.
  • Shared evaluate_holdout.py: pool weighted terms; paired physical twenty-kilometre cluster bootstrap, one thousand draws, ninety-five percent interval. Catalogue recovery is not validation on hidden organizer labels.
  • Compare ablation and a fixed random sparse control; a compatible current-holdout-best implementation is not available in this checkout. No promotion without that comparison.
  • Before any final dots or submission writing, compare the candidate surface to a SHA-verified registry witness with the literal shared uniqueness tool. Any positive duplicate witness is sufficient to stop; it is not sufficient to certify uniqueness. Never scan past a stop to search for a loophole.
  • At most one scientific experiment planned; never more than three or two hours. No competition upload or weekly slot.

Scientific limits

Potential-field inversions are nonunique. Magnetic amplitude changes can reflect magnetization rather than displacement; gravity and basement depth may share inversion priors and are not necessarily independent evidence. This test can falsify a useful ranking but cannot establish a geothermal vent, temperature, permeability, or economic resource. The actual target is new fault traces, as described by the organizer's fault-detection reference notebook, not a vent catalogue.

E2 frozen specification

Inputs (.cache/data/training_features.tif, SHA-pinned): band 12 det_elev, band 2 rtp, band 15 depth_to_base_surf; canary-only comparators band 19 det_elev_slope. Grid/sample/catalogue from .cache/data via shared gems57.grid.load_grid. Vendor code is byte-pinned and unmodified.

  1. valid0 = footprint & finite & |v|<1e18 for every used band. Fill non-valid cells by nearest-valid value from valid0 (fixes the E1 order flaw recorded in docs/irregularities.md: E1 eroded before filling). Output support valid = binary_erosion(valid0, 12) applied after all filtering. No output outside valid.
  2. Elevation: elev_s = gaussian(fill(det_elev), sigma=1). RTP tensor: gradients of gaussian(fill(rtp), 1); tensor components smoothed at sigma=2. Normal angle a = 0.5*atan2(2*Jxy, Jxx-Jyy) (gradient direction, x = column axis). Coherence sqrt((Jxx-Jyy)^2 + 4*Jxy^2)/(Jxx+Jyy+1e-12). Quantize a mod pi to 4 undirected axes (dy,dx): E–W (0,1), NE–SW (1,1) in image coordinates, N–S (1,0), NW–SE (1,-1), by maximum absolute dot product with (sin a, cos a) in (row, col) coordinates.
  3. Step per axis: |shift(elev_s,+2*axis) - shift(elev_s,-2*axis)| by array slicing (no wraparound; 2-px border invalid, already inside erosion). Features: step_oriented (axis chosen by RTP tensor), step_max (maximum over 4 axes, orientation-free control), coherence, basement_edge = |grad(gaussian(fill(depth_to_base),2))|.
  4. Candidate ranking: geometric mean of average-tie ranks of (step_oriented, basement_edge) over valid, re-ranked to [0,1]. No catalogue array is read by the ranking code.
  5. Holdout: shared buffered_component_draw(grid, seed=20, buffer_px=3, boundary_px=12) — identical folds/positives to E1 for paired comparability — plus a confirmatory seed=21 draw reported for the candidate dots and H33 reference only (never used for tuning). Evaluator: shared gems57.evaluate_holdout (gems57-pooled-hide-v2, alpha 0.2, beta 0.8, 300 m triangular kernel), pooled terms, paired 20 km spatial-cluster bootstrap, 1000 draws, seed 70020. Visible faults masked pixel-exactly by the evaluator.
  6. Leakage canaries first: every raw used band and every derived feature, alone, AUC (and inverse) on withheld positives vs 200,000 sampled negatives (seed 70020 sampling as E1). Any max(AUC,1-AUC) > 0.90 stops the run as suspected leakage; no joint interpretation.
  7. Dot emission (primary endpoint): priority Poisson-style thinning — candidates in descending ranking order from the top 400,000 valid pixels, accept if no accepted dot within Euclidean distance < 2.8 px, until 38,000 dots or pool exhaustion. Binary value 1.0. Holdout dots use no catalogue exclusion (evaluator masks visible faults; dots on withheld truth are the signal). Final submission dots additionally require full-catalogue distance > 2 px (disclosed borrowed emission hygiene from the H33 lineage, not claimed novel), refilled to 38,000 by continuing the same priority order.
  8. Arms (all HOLDOUT-DTI, same folds/masks): candidate dots; candidate continuous upper-2% surface (secondary, E1-comparable); ablations step_oriented dots, step_max dots, basement_edge dots (same thin, same target N); random binary dots (38,000 uniform valid pixels, seed 70020, no thinning); reference dot fields scored as-is: H33 B2 (37,654 dots, SHA c55bafc4…), D2.8 (44,090, SHA 3e78737f…), H19-5 solid (121,131, SHA ef2ae808…). Reference caveat: those fields were built with full-catalogue knowledge (incl. distance pruning), so their holdout values are reference measurements on this instrument, not clean generalization estimates; pruning near withheld faults can only remove their near-truth dots (conservative direction), while any catalogue-tuned ranking could favour them. Both directions are stated wherever the numbers are used.
  9. Promotion bars (all required, candidate dots, primary seed-20 draw): (a) pooled DTI point estimate exceeds every arm in (8) including H33; (b) paired 95% CI for candidate-minus-H33 and candidate-minus-random both lie above 0; (c) no canary alarm; (d) uniqueness gates in docs/protocol-revision-uniqueness.md pass on surface and final dots within the disclosed subset scope; (e) shared writer + validator pass on exact bytes. Failure of any bar ⇒ verdict negative, DO NOT SUBMIT, no submission TIFF published as a download. Point-beats-but-CI-crosses-zero ⇒ negative (promising is not promoted).
  10. Budget: E2 is experiment 1 of this session. Wall-clock cap 2 h for E2+E3 combined. No parameter is changed after seeing holdout results.

Protocol revision R1 — dot-overlap definition for continuous registry rasters

Status: explicit revision proposed by this session (2026-10-20), requiring selector acknowledgment before any weekly slot is spent. It does not edit the byte-pinned shared template (vendor/gems57/uniqueness.py is unchanged); it adds a disclosed measurement layer in this repo's own code and reports the template's literal numbers alongside. Per protocol rule 2, the underlying template defect is reported in evidence/upstream_uniqueness_report.md for a single upstream fix; no private fork of the metric or writer is kept.

The defect (measured, not asserted)

The template defines registry "dots" as every finite pixel > 0 (uniqueness._dots). The SHA-verified registry raster 17GEMSDOE:docs/downloads/17GEMSDOE_E-proba-multiscale_20260930T044527Z.tif (SHA-256 ab0a0a62…, re-fetched and re-verified this session) is a continuous probability surface positive on all 5,167,373 footprint cells (minimum ≈ 0.00046). Under the literal definition its 3-px halo covers every allowed cell, so every nonempty candidate — including the family's own shipped H33 champion — has forward overlap 1.0 and must "stop as duplicate". A rule that classifies the champion itself, and every possible nonempty field, as a duplicate of a probability surface cannot be the intended meaning of "70% of your dots within 3 px of one registry raster's dots": continuous surfaces do not have dots until an emission rule is applied. Left unrevised, the rule makes the user's requirement (a unique candidate) logically unsatisfiable, which E1 correctly reported as a stop.

Revision R1 (narrow, anti-copy intent preserved)

For each registry raster, classify by measured support inside the footprint:

  • Sparse / dot field — positive count ≤ 10% of footprint cells: dots = positive pixels, exactly as the literal rule. No change.
  • Dense / continuous surface — positive count > 10%: dots = its top-N footprint pixels by value, where N = the candidate's own dot count (mass-matched emission-equivalent support). Ties broken by stable row-major order. This asks the only well-posed question: "if this surface were emitted at your mass, would your dots coincide with its dots?"

Gates (both stages — pre-placement surface and final dots):

  1. Spearman rank correlation vs every checked registry raster (dense included, full footprint, literal template computation) ≤ 0.90.
  2. Forward 3-px dot overlap ≤ 0.70 vs every sparse raster (literal) and vs every dense raster's R1 top-N dots.
  3. The literal overlap vs dense rasters is still computed and published (expected 1.0); it is labelled literal_dense_overlap_degenerate, never hidden and never averaged away.
  4. Byte/pixel identity vs any checked raster remains an automatic stop.

Scope honesty

This session checks a disclosed subset (highest-risk lineage + dense witness + high-scoring family fields; exact list and SHAs in the E2 evidence), not all 862 inherited-index rasters and not any organizer registry (none is public). A subset pass can reject duplication; it cannot certify complete uniqueness. Promotion therefore means "passes R1 within subset scope; selector must run the complete accessible scan and acknowledge R1 before spending a slot." If the selector rejects R1, every candidate from this session reverts to DO NOT SUBMIT, including any file already downloaded for review.
